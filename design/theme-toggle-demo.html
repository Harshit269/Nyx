THEME TOGGLE

Three ways to switch Nyx between light and dark. Press each one: the old theme fades out over the new one, the same smooth crossfade the app uses. All three also live in Settings.

01 Sidebar footer Recommended

Light · Dark · Auto pill

One click, always visible. Auto follows Windows, so most people never touch it. The black thumb slides to your choice.

02 Top bar corner

Dot-matrix icon button

A round button in the top bar. The dots redraw from a sun to a moon. Compact, but light and dark only, with no Auto.

03 Settings row

Dark mode switch

A plain on/off switch, like Windows Settings. Familiar, but hides the Auto choice. The knob stretches as you press it.

How the switch animatesThe old theme fades out over the new one in about 0.4 seconds, easing in and out, so text stays readable the whole way through. Turn on "reduce motion" in Windows and it swaps instantly instead.
What changes in the appLight is white tiles on light grey. Dark is near-black tiles on pure black, so it looks good on OLED screens. Red appears only on live, urgent things: an incoming call, an unread count, the active link. The title bar goes dark too.
